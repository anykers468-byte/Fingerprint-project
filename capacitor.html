How Does a Capacitor Work?

A capacitor stores electrical energy using two conductive plates separated by an insulating material. Opposite electrical charges build up on the two plates.

Bringing the plates closer together increases capacitance. Increasing their separation decreases it.

The insulating material prevents direct charge flow between the plates. The electric field exists in the space between them.
